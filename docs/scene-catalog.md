# Scene catalog

The desk contains 30 presets. Display numbers are 01–30; protocol IDs are 0–29. The first fifteen IDs and their names remain unchanged; the 2026-09-12 expansion appends three banks of five: Echoes (16–20) continue the existing Signal / Organic / Structures families, Lasers (21–25) are beam-based laser-show effects, and Uncharted (26–30) use vocabularies none of the earlier scenes share. Every scene uses the same authoritative clock, audio features, controls, DROP timing, quality setting, and floor alignment.

The nanoKONTROL2 has eight S buttons. They always select presets 01–08; selecting another group never changes that mapping. REW and FF traverse all 30 presets and wrap at either end. The dashboard and DROP destination menu can select any preset directly. There are no reserved scene placeholders or S9–S30 controls.

M1–M8 one-shots and R1–R8 persistent effects retain the common Orbit, Pulse, Twist, Mirror, Scatter, Strobe, Prism, and Freeze names. STOP and reset clear effects; scene changes retain transport state and restore scene knob defaults. Physical knobs still use pickup after those defaults change.

## Bank notes

- Every preset in the three new banks is a pure function of the authoritative phase, seed, controls, knobs, audio features and scheduled effects, exactly like the earlier fifteen. Nothing integrates local frame time; the pendulum wave, domino fronts, lantern cycles, automaton generations and firework launches are all evaluated analytically from absolute phase, so a rejoining client renders the same picture.
- Shared effects apply to every new preset through one deformation helper: Twist, Mirror, Scatter, distortion and glitch move sampled targets, Prism shifts every bank hue, and Orbit, Pulse, Strobe, Freeze and DROP remain global. Laser emitters stay fixed while their targets deform, so beams remain straight lines.
- Lasers combine a one-pixel additive core line, an instanced soft haze cylinder whose width is a knob, and sprite spots where a beam meets the floor. Sheets and fans use additive vertex-colored triangles. In MR passthrough the luminance-derived alpha keeps only the bright cores and spots.
- AUTOMATON WALL caches two seeded generation blocks per (seed, block, rule, size); a block is recomputed only when the scrolling window enters it. Dead cells are drawn at 3.5 % brightness so they read as a faint grid on the desktop and disappear under the MR alpha threshold.
- Medium quality stays under 15,000 active vertices and six draw calls per preset; Low and High scale strand, beam, ring, dancer, lantern, cell and spark counts.

## Validation boundary

The initial five-scene desktop evidence remains valid historical evidence for those scenes. It is not evidence for the ten additions, and the desktop checks of those ten do not validate the fifteen 2026-09-12 additions. The fifteen new presets were rendered in the production bundle in a headless software-rendered Chromium (SwiftShader WebGL2) at Medium and zoomed High without console errors; that is a rendering check, not a headset, frame-time, or passthrough result. Validate every new preset under silence and synthetic/live features, minimum/maximum controls, each quality tier, rapid scene changes, DROP/STOP, and transparency before performance. A procedural metallic sheen does not imply capture or reflection of the real room.

The user reported successful HMD viewing on 2026-09-09. Agent-operated headset validation remains deferred until the device is explicitly available for this project. Measured stereo MR, transparent Bloom in passthrough, physical scale/alignment, sustained frame rate, and two-headset presentation skew still need explicit evidence for the expanded catalog.
